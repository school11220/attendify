Terms of service

Updated 12 October 2026. These terms describe use of Attendify’s current attendance-planning features.

Using Attendify

Attendify is a personal attendance tracker with subjects, attendance history, timetable, calendar, scenario calculations, optional location-assisted marking, notifications, and data import and export. Keep your account credentials secure and enter information you are entitled to use. Do not access another person’s account, bypass access controls or misuse the service.

Your records and calculations

You are responsible for reviewing your records and correcting mistakes. Calculations depend on the data and attendance targets you enter. Location permission, device accuracy, browser availability and timetable settings affect automated marking and reminders. Attendify does not determine official institutional attendance, excuse absences or guarantee that you meet an institution’s rules. Confirm official attendance requirements and records with your institution.

Import and export

Importing a JSON backup replaces your existing Attendify data. Export a backup and check the imported file first. Protect downloaded JSON, CSV and PDF files because they can contain personal and attendance information.

Authentication and availability

Sign-in depends on Clerk and, if you choose Google, Google. Their services and your browser’s settings affect access. Features may change, and interruptions can occur. Contact the operator about access problems or account deletion. Account and data handling are described in the privacy policy.

Payments

The current application has no purchase, subscription or payment-processing feature. A refund policy is therefore not applicable to its current functionality.
