Privacy policy

Updated 12 October 2026. This page explains how the current Attendify application handles information.

Account and Google sign-in

Clerk provides authentication and session management. Depending on the sign-up method, Clerk processes your email address, name, username, password credentials, verification information and session data. When you choose Google, Google and Clerk process the sign-in exchange, including basic account identity such as your name, email address and profile image. Attendify uses this identity to sign you in and associate your attendance records with your account.

The application implements Google sign-in only. It does not implement access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar or your Google contacts. The current Google sign-in requests openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile.

Attendance and profile data

Attendify stores your entered name, college, semester, attendance targets, subject and faculty names, attendance counts and dated history, timetable slots, synced holidays, subject-specific cancelled-class dates, automation and reminder preferences, and optional college coordinates and geofence radius. This information is stored server-side in your Clerk account’s private metadata under Attendify, with separate calendar and preference entries. API requests use your authenticated user ID to read or update your own records.

Location and notifications

If you enable location features and grant browser permission, the browser obtains your current coordinates to compare your position with the college location you saved. The current location check happens in the browser; the attendance-marking request sends the subject, attendance status and date, rather than your current coordinates. Saved college coordinates are sent to the server and stored with your account. Browser storage records which subjects were automatically marked that day. Optional browser notifications use timetable information to remind you about classes.

Cookies, local storage and caching

Clerk uses cookies and session mechanisms for authentication. Attendify uses local browser storage and a service worker for its interface assets, attendance-marking flags and notification functionality. Removing browser storage does not delete records stored in Clerk.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the application and handles web requests and operational logs. Clerk handles identity and stores attendance metadata. Google handles Google sign-in. Existing dashboard pages load interface libraries, fonts and PDF export libraries from external CDNs, including Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs and the Tailwind CDN; these providers receive ordinary web request information such as your IP address. This repository does not implement advertising, payment processing or an analytics-tracking integration.

Exports, imports and your choices

You can edit profile information, delete subjects and attendance records, and export your account’s attendance data through the profile page. Exports downloaded to your device are under your control. JSON imports replace your existing Attendify records, so review and back up your data first. You can deny or revoke location and notification permissions in your browser. You can also manage Google access through your Google account.

Storage and requests

The application has no automatic time-based deletion schedule. Information persists in your Clerk account metadata until changed or removed. You can remove saved college coordinates, turn off location-assisted attendance and reminders, clear Attendify browser storage, delete your entered attendance data while retaining your sign-in account, or permanently delete your Clerk account and its stored metadata from the profile page. Data and account deletion require a recent sign-in and typed confirmation. These controls do not delete downloaded exports, browser data on other devices, or provider operational logs. Contact the operator about remaining-data requests or privacy questions. Deleting downloaded files or revoking Google access alone does not delete your Attendify records.
